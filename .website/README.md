Carry Quest is a rainbow-unicorn arithmetic adventure! Solve addition, subtraction, and multiplication problems to collect colorful horn pieces, choose perks, and prepare for a final boss.

My goal here was to help kids learn arithmetic mechanics in a fun way that scales to any age. Not only do you get a beep when you make a mistake, you get a why to go along with it tracked in an insights tab. 

For kids, it's an arithmetic tool, but the higher levels and endless mode should challenge the fastest fingers out there. 

Features:
- Rapid-fire math practice for kids
- Almost rhythm game-like engagement for the mechanical game fans out there
- Feedback on common errors and sticking points collected through telemetry, so you can see where your issues are and improve. 
- Exportable json with all local run data

Instructions: 
Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard. Check Unlimited for an endless trail with three lives and no final boss.
How to play:
- Pick a connected level on the map. Its symbols show the operations, its score shows difficulty, and its colored half shows the horn pieces you can earn. Starred challenges award two colors.
- Tap a level or press 1–5 to select its lane. Press Enter or the same number again to begin.
- Work from right to left, entering one answer digit at a time.
- Carrying? Swipe up or down anywhere in the problem area to adjust the carry by one. Keep going until it is correct, then enter the final digit if needed. Follow the arrows to borrow. Keyboard arrows and on-screen arrow buttons work too.
- Watch the step timer! Multiplication gives two extra seconds per step. Later levels also have a whole-problem timer.
- Finish the level to earn its difficulty score minus your mistakes, collect horn pieces, and choose one of three perks by tapping or pressing 1–3.
- Tap PAUSE anytime to review the rules
- Clear ten levels to face the ten-question boss. Collecting more horn colors slows its difficulty growth—a complete rainbow keeps it at difficulty 6!
